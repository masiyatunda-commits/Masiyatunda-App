◉ Kuhusu MilkyWay

MilkyWay ni social platform ya Masiyatunda yenye kauli Freedom · Truth · Light.

Watumiaji wanaweza kufungua akaunti, kupost picha/video, kufuatana, kutoa Signals, kuandika Echoes, kuhifadhi posts, kutafuta watu, kupokea notifications na kuripoti content.

MilkyWay ina database yake ndani ya Supabase na imeandaliwa ili baadaye itumike pia na Flutter/FlutterFlow kupitia backend hiyo hiyo.
